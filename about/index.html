06 / ABOUT

Evidence people can actually use.

I am an international-relations researcher and computational social scientist focused on international security, transparency, public opinion, and human rights.

Research across institutions, classrooms, and policy spaces.

The common thread is practical: finding structure in complex information and communicating it clearly enough for others to question, explore, and use.

That has meant building an arms-transfer dashboard from more than 25,000 fields of reported information, writing country-level transparency analysis, coordinating public-opinion research, evaluating social programs across four countries, teaching R and Power BI, and presenting evidence to diplomats and government officials.

I am a Fulbright Scholar pursuing an MS in Applied Quantitative Methods and Social Analysis at Northeastern University, and the youngest member of the ATT Monitor Steering Committee.
