06 / ABOUT

International relations, through data.

I am a researcher from Argentina working at the intersection of computational social science and international affairs.

My work began in public opinion research and now spans international security, transparency, and human rights.

I combine substantive training in International Relations with quantitative and computational methods. I have coordinated survey research, evaluated social programs in four countries, analyzed arms transfer reporting, and taught R and Power BI.

At the ATT Monitor, I designed an interactive dashboard from more than 25,000 fields of reported information and presented it to diplomats and government officials. I now study political text and networks while pursuing an MS in Applied Quantitative Methods and Social Analysis at Northeastern University as a Fulbright Scholar.
