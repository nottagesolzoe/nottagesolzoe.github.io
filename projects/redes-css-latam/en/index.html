02 / A STRUCTURAL OVERVIEW

What the observed
network shows.

Recorded professionals by country

The same people as the map and active filters, across all categories. Unique people; institutional country, not nationality.

Source: public profiles in the directory. Uneven coverage prevents comparisons of the actual size of national fields.

Institutions with the most linked profiles

Documented affiliations of the same selected people. Each person is counted once per institution and may have multiple affiliations. The ten institutions with the most profiles are shown.

Source: institutional profiles. This is not a ranking of quality or research output.

Analysis details

The coauthorship network is undirected. Degree counts distinct coauthors; strength sums shared works. Displayed indicators are recalculated for the active selection. Betweenness uses unweighted shortest paths and is normalized. Weighted eigenvector centrality is calculated only within the largest component. Communities use Louvain with resolution 1 and seed 42. These results describe the observed corpus and are not prestige rankings.

Chart design: direct labels, sorted bars and a zero baseline. Data cutoff: October 20, 2026.
