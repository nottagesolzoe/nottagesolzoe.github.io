02 / AFFILIATIONS

Professionals by institution

Where do the people in the directory work? The chart includes the ten institutions with the most recorded professionals. The same person may appear at several institutions.

03 / MULTIPLE AFFILIATIONS

People linked to multiple institutions

Which institutions share people? The network identifies people recorded at two or more institutions through affiliation, teaching or appointments. Lines represent these links, and outlined circles represent institutions. Personal nodes are anonymous. These links do not measure collaboration.

Selecting a country filters people by their institutional base. The network retains their links to institutions in other countries so these connections remain visible.
