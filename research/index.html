ONGOING RESEARCH

RecetarIA

A collection of computational methods and AI recipes for social science research. Each recipe sets out a concrete task, the required materials, steps, prompts and checks for reviewing the result. The learning path moves from simpler to more complex tasks, from preparing data to comparing models, and offers routes for further learning. I am developing a reusable skills library so people can take these recipes into their preferred AI assistant, such as ChatGPT or Claude.
