CO-FOUNDER · ISSN 3072-8916

REsIDeS

I co-founded REsIDeS, an academic journal created by young scholars to support peers in producing knowledge on Sustainable Development. We built it as an interdisciplinary, federal, digital, and open-access platform, with a Scientific Committee representing the three pillars of sustainable development and members of the UNESCO Chair at UdeSA, alongside a student-led Editorial Committee.

The journal publishes one annual issue and connects contributors and readers across Argentina and the Spanish-speaking region. After graduating, I returned to the University of San Andrés as an assistant professor, teaching introductory International Relations and methodological courses.
